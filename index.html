SELECTED RESEARCH

From perception to physical interaction.

Selected work from my previous research in robot perception, manipulation, and teleoperation.

01 · ROBOT VISION

Zero-Shot 6D Pose Tracking for Sequential Robot Manipulation

A perception pipeline for unseen objects combining open-vocabulary detection, Segment Anything Model segmentation, online video segmentation, and BundleSDF for continuous 6D pose tracking from RGB-D observations.

SAMBundleSDFRGB-D6D Pose Tracking

Presented at ICCAS 2024 · Poster Session

02 · GRASPING

Unknown-Object Grasping with SAM & Contact-GraspNet

Text-guided object detection and segmentation were combined with RGB-D grasp-pose estimation, followed by coordinate transformation and real-robot grasp execution.

Grounding DINOSAMContact-GraspNet

03 · 3D PERCEPTION

Object Orientation Estimation with YOLO-OBB & TRIAD

Estimated connector orientation using oriented bounding-box detection, point-cloud surface normals, a camera reference vector, and the TRIAD attitude-determination method.

YOLO-OBBPoint CloudTRIAD

Presented at ICRA 2024 · LBR Poster Session

04 · MANIPULATION

6D Pose-Based Recovery from Rotational Slip

A proof-of-concept experiment from my M.S. thesis used BundleSDF to track the 6D pose of a grasped object after an intentional rotational slip. The estimated rotation was used to realign the robot before peg-in-hole insertion; final translational positioning was completed through teleoperation.

BundleSDF6D Pose TrackingSlip RecoveryPeg-in-Hole

M.S. Thesis Experiment

05 · TELEOPERATION

Haptic Teleoperation & Autonomous Control

A ROS 2-based system using a Force Dimension Omega 7 and Franka Research 3 to evaluate haptic teleoperation and autonomous control. The operator switched between the two control modes using a foot pedal. In an avatar-system setup, HMD orientation was also used to control a remote camera viewpoint.
